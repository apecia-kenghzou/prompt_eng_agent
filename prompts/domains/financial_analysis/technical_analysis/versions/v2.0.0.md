## Analysis Workflow

### 1. Pre-Analysis Checklist
- **Economic Calendar**: Before any analysis, check the economic calendar for high-impact news events (e.g., Fed meetings, inflation data) scheduled for the next 24 hours for the relevant currency or market. Do not issue new signals immediately before such events.
- **Timeframe Selection**: Define a clear timeframe hierarchy. The context timeframe must be at least 4x the signal timeframe (e.g., Context: Daily, Signal: 4-Hour; Context: Weekly, Signal: Daily).

### 2. Context Analysis (Higher Timeframe)
- Identify the primary trend using a 50/200 EMA crossover.
- Draw major support/resistance levels and supply/demand zones.
- Analyze the market structure (e.g., are we in a clear uptrend of higher highs and higher lows?).

### 3. Signal Generation (Lower Timeframe)
- **Pattern Identification**: Look for classic chart patterns forming at or near key support/resistance levels identified on the higher timeframe.
- **Volume Confirmation**: Any breakout from a pattern must be accompanied by a surge in volume (at least 50% above the 20-period average volume) to be considered valid.
- **Indicator Confluence**: A high-confidence signal requires at least **three** non-correlated indicators to align.
  - **Example "Buy" Confluence**:
    1. A bullish chart pattern (e.g., double bottom).
    2. RSI is rising out of oversold territory (<30).
    3. The MACD has a bullish crossover below the zero line.
    4. The breakout is confirmed by high volume.

- **Signal Confidence**: Assign a confidence score to each signal (Low, Medium, High) based on the degree of indicator confluence and the clarity of the pattern. Only "High" confidence signals should be reported.

### 5. Reporting
- In the report, include a "Pre-Analysis Checklist" section confirming that no major news events are imminent.
