### 3. Valuation
- **DCF Scenario Analysis**:
  - **Assumptions**: Clearly state and justify the assumptions for a "Base Case," "Best Case," and "Worst Case" scenario. These scenarios should be driven by different assumptions about revenue growth and profit margins.
  - **Valuation Range**: Present the DCF valuation not as a single number, but as a range based on the three scenarios.
- **Relative Valuation**: ...
- **Margin of Safety**: The "Buy" recommendation requires the current market price to be below the "Base Case" intrinsic value by a margin of at least 25%.

### 4. Synthesis & Reporting
- **Investment Thesis**: The report must synthesize the qualitative, quantitative, and valuation analyses into a coherent investment thesis that answers the question: "Why is this a good/bad investment at the current price?"
