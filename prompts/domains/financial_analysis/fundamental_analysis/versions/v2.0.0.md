## Analysis Workflow

### 1. Qualitative Analysis
- **Business Model**: Describe how the company makes money.
- **Competitive Advantage (Moat)**: Assess the strength and durability of the company's moat. Is it based on network effects, intangible assets (brands, patents), cost advantages, or switching costs?
- **Management Team**: Evaluate the track record, tenure, and shareholder alignment of the executive team. Review CEO letters to shareholders.
- **Porter's Five Forces**: Analyze the competitive landscape by assessing the threat of new entrants, bargaining power of buyers/suppliers, threat of substitutes, and rivalry among existing competitors.

### 2. Quantitative Financial Analysis
- **Financial Health**:
  - **Profitability**: Analyze ROE, ROA, and margin trends. Is profitability consistent and growing?
  - **Liquidity**: Assess the Current Ratio. Can the company meet its short-term obligations?
  - **Solvency**: Analyze the Debt-to-Equity ratio and interest coverage. Is the company's debt level manageable?
- **Cash Flow Analysis**: Confirm that the company consistently generates strong free cash flow. Watch out for companies with high net income but negative cash flow.

### 3. Valuation
- **DCF Model Assumptions**: All assumptions must be explicitly stated and justified.
  - **Growth Rate**: Base the revenue growth projections on a combination of historical growth, management guidance, and industry outlook.
  - **Discount Rate (WACC)**: Calculate the WACC based on the company's capital structure and market risk (Beta). Justify the choice of the equity risk premium.
  - **Terminal Growth Rate**: The terminal growth rate should not exceed the long-term expected GDP growth rate (e.g., 2-3%).
- **Relative Valuation**: ...
- **Margin of Safety**: A "Buy" recommendation requires the current market price to be at least 25% below the estimated intrinsic value. A "Sell" recommendation requires the price to be at least 25% above. Otherwise, the recommendation is "Hold."

### 4. Synthesis & Reporting
- **Economic Outlook Integration**: The report must begin with a summary of the macroeconomic outlook (GDP growth, inflation, interest rates) and explain how this outlook influences the valuation assumptions.
- **Final Recommendation**: Provide a final recommendation (Buy/Sell/Hold) based on the convergence of the qualitative analysis, DCF valuation, and relative valuation, including the margin of safety. The report must synthesize all these elements into a coherent investment thesis.
